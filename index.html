Do we need new software?

Usually, no. We start with the tools you already use and check what they can already do. Any additional software and costs are discussed before a build.

What stays with our team?

Decisions, approvals, and anything outside the agreed rules. We define what can run automatically and what needs a person before the workflow goes live.

Where does AI actually help?

Reading varied documents, organizing information, and preparing drafts. Clear, predictable tasks often need straightforward automation instead. We use the simplest approach that fits.

How do you price the work?

The build is scoped and quoted before work starts. Hosting, monitoring, fixes, and ongoing improvements can be covered by a separate care plan. See our engagement options.
